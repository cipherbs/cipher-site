## Branding

Arquivo: `media/area-branding.webp`.

Prompt final:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: high-resolution service illustration for CIPHER's visual identity and branding offering. Premium editorial still life of a cohesive visual identity system, arranged elegantly on a very light gray seamless studio surface: matte ivory business cards, a cream brand-guideline booklet open to pages with an orange modular geometric symbol and restrained neutral type-line placeholders, a small orange rounded square color swatch, a pale gray paper folder, and one simple ivory packaging box. Use the same original geometric orange symbol consistently across these items, but no actual text or legible brand names. It is an illustrative fictional identity, not a real client project. One black and white symbol sketch sheet partially visible emphasizes creation and refinement of a brand. Photorealistic high quality paper grain, embossing and restrained orange foil details; soft natural directional lighting and fine contact shadows. Contemporary minimalist art direction. Palette white, warm gray, ivory and vermilion orange, no unrelated colors, no black backgrounds, no watermarks, no third-party logos. Balanced front three-quarter composition with comfortable breathing space. Wide landscape 3:2, crisp and excellent detail.
